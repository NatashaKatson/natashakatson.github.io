Where I come in

What I Fix

I help B2B SaaS teams solve the positioning, launch, and collaboration problems that make it harder to bring products to market.

Broken GTM & Launch Processes

Launches keep slipping. Ownership is unclear. Product ships before sales and marketing are ready.

I help you find where your go-to-market process breaks down and build a repeatable approach, from launch planning and responsibilities to sales readiness and customer communications.

Positioning & Messaging That Isn’t Working

Your product is good, but prospects struggle to understand why they should choose it.

I help you define who your product is for, what makes it different, and why that difference matters. Then I turn that positioning into messaging your team can use across your website, campaigns, and sales conversations.

Misaligned Teams

Product, marketing, and sales are working toward different priorities. Decisions get revisited, handoffs get missed, and customers hear different stories.

I help teams clarify ownership, agree on priorities, and build better ways of working together so everyone understands what’s launching, who it’s for, and how to communicate its value.

AI Messaging Lost in the Hype

Your AI features get described as “powerful” or “intelligent,” but customers still don’t know what they can actually do with them.

I help you explain what your AI does, how customers can use it, and what makes it different, with clear positioning and practical examples grounded in what the product delivers.

Sales Teams Missing What They Need

Sales reps struggle to explain differentiation, answer competitive questions, or find the right materials for a conversation.

I turn product knowledge and competitive insights into battlecards, talk tracks, and playbooks that help your team communicate value and handle objections confidently.

Users Who Don't Stick Around

Users sign up but don't activate. They try once but don't return. I diagnose where onboarding breaks down, identify the moments that matter for activation, and build frameworks to improve time-to-value and long-term retention.

FAQ

What does a B2B SaaS product marketing consultant help with?

A product marketing consultant helps connect what a product does with why customers should choose it. My work focuses on positioning, messaging, go-to-market planning, product launches, sales enablement, and alignment across product, marketing, and sales.

Do you work with AI products?

Yes. I help teams position and market AI products by explaining what they do, who they’re for, and what makes them different.

I also help teams use AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT in their day-to-day work. This includes setting up workflows, creating reusable skills, and integrating AI into product marketing processes, from research and messaging to launch planning and content creation.

Are you available?

I’m available for consulting projects. I’m based in Vancouver, BC.
