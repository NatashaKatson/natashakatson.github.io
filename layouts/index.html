Where I come in

What I Fix

Most GTM problems aren't strategy problems — they're alignment, process, and clarity problems.

Broken GTM & Launch Processes

Launches keep slipping. Nobody owns the timeline. Product ships without sales knowing what to say. I diagnose where your release process breaks down and build the frameworks that get product, marketing, and sales moving together — and keep them moving after I leave.

Misaligned Teams

Product builds what sales can't sell. Marketing creates content nobody uses. I find the root cause of the disconnect — usually a combination of unclear ownership, poor communication rhythms, and missing shared language — and fix it at the source.

Positioning & Messaging That Isn't Working

Your product is good, but the market doesn't see it that way. Deals stall, prospects say "interesting but not right now," and nobody can explain what you do in one sentence. I help you find the positioning that actually resonates — and build the messaging that makes it stick.

AI & GenAI Product Marketing

AI products all sound the same — "AI-powered" everything. The companies winning are the ones who can articulate what their AI actually does differently. I specialize in cutting through the noise to position AI products with clear, credible differentiation.

Sales That Can't Close

When deals stall, it's often not the leads — it's unclear value communication and missing frameworks. I build the battlecards, talk tracks, and playbooks that help your sales team articulate differentiation consistently and handle competitive objections confidently.

Users Who Don't Stick Around

Users sign up but don't activate. They try once but don't return. I diagnose where onboarding breaks down, identify the moments that matter for activation, and build frameworks to improve time-to-value and long-term retention.
