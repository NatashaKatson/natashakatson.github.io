Areas of Expertise

Translating complex B2B SaaS products into clear, compelling market stories

Go-to-Market Strategy

Companies often lack clear GTM frameworks - who to target first, how to reach them, and what messaging will resonate. I diagnose gaps in your strategy and build actionable roadmaps that align product, marketing, and sales.

Product Positioning & Messaging

Your product needs to answer: who it's for, what alternatives it replaces, and why it's different. I help you move beyond feature lists to positioning that resonates and messaging that converts.

Generative AI

AI products often sound identical - "AI-powered" everything. I specialize in positioning AI/GenAI products with clear differentiation, helping you articulate unique value beyond the technology itself.

Product Onboarding & Adoption

Users sign up but don't activate. They try once but don't return. I diagnose where your onboarding breaks down and create frameworks to improve activation and retention.

Sales Enablement & Process Improvement

When sales struggles to close, it's often not the leads - it's unclear value communication or missing frameworks. I build battlecards, talk tracks, and playbooks that help sales teams articulate differentiation consistently.

Competitive Analysis

Understanding your competitive landscape means knowing what alternatives customers consider and why they choose (or don't choose) you. I create frameworks that inform positioning, messaging, and sales strategy.

Building PMM Functions from Scratch

Hiring your first PMM without clear scope, priorities, or success metrics leads to misalignment. I help early-stage companies define the role, build foundational processes, and set up their first PMM for success.
