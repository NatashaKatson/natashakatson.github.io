Sources & historical accuracy

Dates & storiesDates before about 500 BC are approximate; some later dates are debated. Dinosaur entries use approximate fossil ages and representative present-day fossil regions, with museum and research sources. Entries link to their sources, and people's dates are checked against Wikidata.
